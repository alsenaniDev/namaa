Build a complete offline application for managing personal income, monthly commitments, expenses, and salary net balance.

Important rules:
- Do not use a backend.
- Do not use Firebase.
- Do not use external APIs.
- The app must store all data locally inside the app.
- The app must work offline.
- The main language of the app is Arabic.
- The UI must support RTL layout.
- The app should be modern, clean, simple, and easy to use.
- The first version must be a working MVP, not just UI screens.
- Choose the best technology that works well inside Replit.
- Do not mention or depend on any mobile-specific framework unless needed.

App idea:
The application helps users organize their monthly financial life. The user can enter their monthly salary and other income sources, then add monthly commitments such as loans, rent, bills, subscriptions, and other fixed payments. The user can also add variable expenses. The app calculates the remaining net amount from the salary and shows a clear monthly financial summary.

Main features:

1. Initial Setup
Create a first-time setup screen where the user enters:
- User name
- Preferred currency, default SAR
- Main monthly salary
- Monthly saving goal, optional
- Financial month start day, default 1

After setup, store the data locally.

2. Dashboard
Create a dashboard screen that shows:
- Total monthly income
- Total monthly commitments
- Total monthly expenses
- Net remaining amount
- Commitment percentage from income
- Expense percentage from income
- Suggested saving amount
- Financial health status

Formula:
Net Remaining = Total Income - Total Commitments - Total Expenses

Financial health status:
- If commitments are less than 30% of income: ممتاز
- If commitments are between 30% and 50%: متوسط
- If commitments are between 50% and 70%: خطر
- If commitments are more than 70%: حرج جدًا

Show this status clearly with a card and a simple Arabic message.
